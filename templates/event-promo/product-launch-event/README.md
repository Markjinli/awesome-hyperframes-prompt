# Product Launch Event — Keynote Opener

> 30-second Apple-style keynote opener. Dramatic product reveal driven entirely by light. The product stays hidden as a silhouette for the first 20 seconds, then fully reveals in a climactic final act.

---

## Use Cases

- Hardware and software product launch keynotes
- Annual brand conference opening sequences
- Flagship product announcement videos
- Investor day and shareholder meeting openers
- Premium brand reveal campaigns

---

## Key Techniques

| Technique | Where | Why |
|-----------|-------|-----|
| **Light-driven narrative** | All scenes | A single light source moves, expands, and reveals -- the audience follows the light, not the product |
| **Silhouette suspense** | First 20s | Product shown only as a dark silhouette against a glowing edge -- builds anticipation through withholding |
| **Minimalist stage** | All scenes | Pure black background with a single light source -- mimics a real keynote stage |
| **Dramatic reveal timing** | Final 10s | Product fully illuminates only after sustained tension -- earns the audience's attention |

---

## Customization

1. Replace the product name and description
2. Add your product render or photo as the hero asset
3. Adjust the reveal timing -- when does the silhouette resolve into the full product?
4. Modify light color temperature: warm (amber/gold) vs cool (white/blue)
