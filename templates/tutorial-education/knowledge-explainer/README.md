# Knowledge Explainer — "How AI Actually Works"

> 30-second educational explainer video. Clean blue-and-white editorial aesthetic with diagram-like layouts. Vox / Kurzgesagt style adapted for professional audiences. Five scenes progressing from hook to key takeaway.

---

## Use Cases

- Educational YouTube content and explainer series intros
- Company internal training and onboarding videos
- Conference talk openers and session introductions
- Product education for complex technical topics
- Newsletter or blog video summaries
- Science communication and STEM outreach content

---

## Key Techniques

| Technique | Where | Why |
|-----------|-------|-----|
| **Character-by-character typewriter** | Scene 1 title | Title types out at 50ms/char with blinking cursor — builds anticipation and signals an explainer |
| **Definition card + keyword badges** | Scene 2 | Left sidebar with staggered slide-in pill badges + bordered quote card — classic editorial layout |
| **Sequential flowchart build** | Scene 3 | Data -> Learn -> Predict -> Improve steps build with GSAP `back.out` pop-ins — makes ML digestible |
| **Side-by-side analogy panels** | Scene 4 | Human Learning vs Machine Learning with synchronized checkmark steps — concrete comparison |
| **Dark-scene contrast** | Scene 5 | Only dark scene (deep blue gradient) — creates memorability and signals "this is the key takeaway" |
| **Equation reveal** | Scene 5 | "AI = Data + Patterns + Iteration" builds with staggered word groups in distinct colors — closing formula |
| **Floating geometric shapes** | Scene 1 | CSS circles, squares, triangles drift with GSAP `sine.inOut` — systems-and-patterns visual metaphor |

---

## Customization

1. Replace `"How AI Actually Works"` with your topic title
2. Change `"It's not magic. It's math."` subtitle to your hook phrase
3. Update flowchart steps in Scene 3 to match your topic's process
4. Modify the side-by-side analogy in Scene 4 for your domain
5. Adjust the closing equation in Scene 5
6. Update colors: `#3B82F6` (accent blue), `#F59E0B` (highlight), `#1E3A5F` (dark scene)
