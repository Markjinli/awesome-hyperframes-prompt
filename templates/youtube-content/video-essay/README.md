# Video Essay Opener — "The Death of the Internet"

> 20-second documentary-style video essay opener. Dark academia aesthetic with antique gold accents and archive photography. Four meditative scenes: archive grid, provocative question, chapter cards, and glitched invitation.

---

## Use Cases

- YouTube video essay openers (culture, tech, history, media criticism)
- Documentary film title sequences
- Academic presentation openers
- Deep-dive content series branding
- Thought-leadership video essays

---

## Key Techniques

| Technique | Where | Why |
|-----------|-------|-----|
| **Archive photo grid** | Scene 1 | 3x3 desaturated photo grid fades in cell-by-cell from center outward -- scrapbook/archival feel |
| **Ken Burns drift** | Scene 1, Scene 4 | Randomized `scale` and `transform-origin` pushes and pans -- documentary movement |
| **Gold rule framing** | Scene 2 | Thin #D4AF37 horizontal rules draw in from edges -- frames the provocative question like a book title page |
| **Color transition emphasis** | Scene 2 | "PROMISED" shifts from #F5F5F0 to #D4AF37 -- draws the eye to the key word |
| **Chapter cards** | Scene 3 | Roman numerals (I, II, III) at 120px with gold vertical divider -- elegant chapter preview |
| **Glitch artifacts** | Scene 4 | CSS `clip-path` polygon shifts + `translate` jitter -- digital decay visual metaphor |
| **Film grain + light leaks** | Global | Subtle texture overlay for analog/documentary authenticity |
| **24fps cadence** | Global | Lower frame rate for filmic, contemplative feel |

---

## Customization

1. Replace `"The Death of the Internet"` essay title and subtitle
2. Swap the guiding question `"WHAT HAPPENED TO THE INTERNET WE WERE PROMISED?"`
3. Update chapter titles and date ranges (I. THE PROMISE, II. THE CAPTURE, III. THE WASTELAND)
4. Modify closing line `"The internet isn't dead. But it's on life support."`
5. Adjust colors: `#1A1A1A` (obsidian bg), `#D4AF37` (antique gold), `#F5F5F0` (parchment) -> your palette
6. Replace archive photo references with imagery relevant to your essay topic
