Key Milestones
March 2020
Global lockdowns begin. 85% of companies mandate remote work overnight, triggering the largest workplace shift in modern history.
June 2021
Hybrid work models emerge. 63% of high-growth companies adopt remote-first policies. Office vacancy rates hit record highs.
September 2022
Return-to-office mandates begin. Tension between employers and employees on flexibility. "Quiet quitting" enters the lexicon.
January 2024
Remote work infrastructure matures. VR collaboration tools reach enterprise adoption. Async communication becomes standard practice.
March 2026
74% of knowledge workers are hybrid or fully remote. Remote work is no longer an experiment — it is the new default.
